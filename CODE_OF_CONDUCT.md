# Beam Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Beam a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, ability, or
personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still addressing harmful impact.
- Welcome first-time contributors and explain project conventions.
- Keep reviews focused on correctness, maintainability, safety, and user value.
- Respect privacy. Do not post personal paths, IP addresses, credentials,
  private media, or identifying logs.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Spam, deceptive contributions, plagiarism, or attempts to game contribution
  events.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional collaborative
  environment.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and
other contributions that violate this code. They may issue a warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when
necessary.

To report a conduct concern without publishing sensitive details, open a brief
GitHub Discussion asking for a private maintainer contact channel. Do not
include private evidence in the public post. Immediate threats or platform
abuse should also be reported through GitHub's abuse-reporting tools.

Maintainers will make reasonable efforts to review reports promptly, protect
the reporter's privacy, and respond proportionately.

## Scope

This code applies in the Beam repository and in public spaces where someone is
representing the project or its community.
